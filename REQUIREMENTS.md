## Core Features

1. Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

    - Customer contact management
    - Customer history tracking
    - Customer communication tools
    - Public business profile creation

2. Job Management & Scheduling

    - Creating schedules and job scheduling capabilities
    - Route planning and optimization
    - Easy scheduling and route planning capabilities
    - Job status tracking

3. Estimation & Quoting

    - Generating estimates
    - Customized price lists creation
    - Matrix pricing (beta feature) - square footage-based pricing calculations
    - Professional estimate templates

4. Invoicing & Billing

    - Completing invoices and professional invoice generation
    - Email invoices capability
    - Automated billing processes
    - Invoice customization

5. Payment Processing

    - Payment processing and tracking
    - Integration with Stripe payment platform
    - Online payment acceptance
    - Payment history tracking

6. Time Tracking & Employee Management

    - Timesheets and time tracking
    - Time Clock, Clock in/Clock out functionality
    - Employee management
    - Work hour tracking

7. Equipment & Inventory Management

    - Equipment tracking
    - Equipment maintenance schedules
    - Inventory management

8. Financial Management

    - Expense management and tracking
    - Bank account integration via Plaid
    - Financial reporting
    - Accounting integration

9. Specialized Industry Features

    - Chemical Applications tracking (for lawn care)
    - Snow Plowing and Snow Removal management
    - Measure Lot Sizes Using Satellite Images
    - Property management tools

10. Mobile & Desktop Applications

    - Mobile app for iOS and Android
    - Desktop application for Mac and Windows
    - Cloud-based accessibility
    - Offline functionality

11. Communication & Documentation

    - Email integration
    - SMS/text messaging capabilities
    - Photo upload for completed work
    - Business logo integration
    - File attachment system

## Business Model & Pricing

### Free Tier

-   Sponsored ad business model similar to Google and Facebook enabling users to benefit from the software for free
-   Free plan includes all the basic features needed to run a landscaping or lawn care business
-   Basic features available at no cost

### Premium Features

-   Optional premium features for larger companies requiring more sophisticated functionality
-   Advanced reporting and analytics
-   Enhanced customization options
-   Priority support

## Target Market

-   Small and medium-sized companies in the landscaping industry
-   Landscaping companies, lawn care businesses, and snow removal contractors of all sizes
-   Solopreneurs and small lawn care teams operating on tight budgets

## Value Proposition

-   Targeting the landscaping industry, one of the last industries yet to be "software enabled" with most companies running on pencil and paper

## Technical Infrastructure

-   Cloud-based SaaS platform
-   Stripe integration for payments
-   Plaid integration for banking
-   Mobile and desktop applications
-   Email and SMS capabilities
